Art module (`game/tools/art/kiwi_cat.gd`, run headless with `--script res://tools/art/kiwi_cat.gd`):
the source ships an alpha channel with alpha-0 corners (the curled kitten is cut out already), so
the alpha channel is the background mask; a border flood fill at per-channel tolerance 24/255
(sample the border colours, fill inward from every border pixel) is the documented fallback for an
opaque-corner re-export and is skipped for this source. Tight crop to the body (alpha > 16 = the
only hitbox-eligible pixels), bottom-anchored on a power-of-two 512x512 canvas with a >= 2 px
transparent margin on every side, and a uniform 1.22x brightness lift on every frame (the A8
fog-contrast hard rule: the source's mean opaque colour washed out against the #021416 fog at
40 u - the art was lifted, the threshold was not). Derived frames are pure functions of the base
(spawn = 1.25x lift, idle_chase = 96% vertical squash about the bottom line, telegraph = 30%
blend toward danger red #FF2A2A at 1.15x lift) - no RNG, no wall clock, byte-stable rebuilds.

HITBOXES hard rule: a frame's `body_rect` in sprites.json (tight bbox of alpha > 16) is the
only solid part of the sprite; the transparent padding and margins never hit.
