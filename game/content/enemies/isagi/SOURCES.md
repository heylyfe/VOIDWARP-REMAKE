Derivation (every frame is a deterministic overlay of the base body - no randomness anywhere):
`idle_00` base idle, `spawn_00` brightened + cyan-white lerp (under the binding common_fade),
`telegraph_00` red rim baked around the silhouette (under the binding red_rim_flash),
`shoot_00` the kick pose, `recover_00` brightness x0.7 (the binding dim), `shot_00` the ball,
`fire_00/01/02` the legacy `fire` animation (animations[0]): idle, squash 0.90/1.06, shoot.
Recipe and its [CONTESTABLE] art numbers: DECISIONS.md D-ART-ISAGI-1.

HITBOXES hard rule: a frame's `body_rect` in sprites.json (tight bbox of alpha > 16) is the
only solid part of the sprite; the transparent padding and margins never hit.
