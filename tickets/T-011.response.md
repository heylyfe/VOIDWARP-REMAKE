## The Deepwater walkway defect (the DONE-WHEN gap)

Measured under the implementation of D-T011-1 (linear radiance mix + stated emissions + sRGB encode
-> WCAG): deepwater walkway_light `#2A5BFF` vs fog `#021416` at 60 u = **2.737** vs the 3.0
threshold. The letter-sRGB reading (T-014c's naive) gives **1.73** for the same row; both its
emission-less variants also fail (2.28 / lower). #2A5BFF is a saturated blue and WCAG-luminance-poor
- no defensible reading of section 12.3's own formula reaches 3.0 for it, which is why T-014c's
prediction holds against the reference Deepwater data, not just their naive prototype. Row
candidates measured for T-054 (hue preserved, lightness lifted): `#3A74FF` -> 3.394, `#4A84FF` ->
3.925, `#5A9AFF` -> 4.776. I changed no colour and no threshold; expect the gate green again after
T-054's one-file palette edit.

## Deviations

- **fog_check emits on Deepwater exit 1** -> the gate step is FAIL, not OK: unavoidable given
  "implement section 12.3 exactly" + "report genuine palette failures" (the two brief clauses
  conflict; the KNOWN-RISK clause decided it). Everything else in the gate stayed green; run_all
  now runs 4 tests (the new test_mesh.gd at the request of the done-when's timing/triangle
  proofs; the run_all step consumed it without extra gate code).
- **Checker/grain/sigil live in shaders, not baked textures**: the exact grain formula is
  per-texel in world coordinates, so a repeatable texture cannot express it; computing it in the
  world-space shader keeps the letter of section 12.2 (8 px/u nearest virtual texel grid) and
  makes the greedy merge texture-free. The CPU-side reference `grain_factor()` pins the formula
  for tests; negative texel indices normalize into the unsigned 32-bit domain (the uint() wrap in
  the shader agrees).
- **Support spikes**: fixed stepped profile, no dressing RNG (section 12.4's allowance unused);
  skip rule concretised as D-T011-5 (
bbox <= 7x7, 9-column ring check, hub excluded by role).
- **Emission factors in fog_check**: checker lights use the section 12.2 "0.35 x albedo" emission
  (total radiance factor 1.35); tripmine particles use the 2.0 emission stated in their runtime
  spec (research/fable-tickets.md, the particles row); rim 1.5 / gold 2.0 / beacon open 4.0 come
  from section 12.2 itself. These are parts of the roles' on-screen colour, not adjustments.
- **game/shots/** is a new directory (not in the FLEET section 3 tree); the PNGs live under the
  project root because res://shots globalizes there - flagged in case the owner wants shots/
