# B10 response: the sequential outside-in collapse (D-B10-1)

## What changed and why

The owner wanted the collapse to work like a shrinking safe zone. It used to be one global fall:
every non-hub tile went 5 s after the beacon opened. Now zones crack and fall one at a time,
from the outside in.

- `game/data/collapse.json` (new): `crack_to_fall_s` 5.0, `gap_after_fall_s` 10.0,
  `zone_width_u` 16.0, `zone_min` 1, `zone_max` 6, with a `_doc` in the
  `offering_schedule.json` style.
- `game/sim/collapse.gd`: rewritten to run one schedule per zone. `create(level, cfg={})`
  builds the zones; `start` cracks zone 0; `tick` drops each zone on its fall tick and cracks
  the next one after the gap (a while-loop, so a 0 s gap also works). New read-only queries:
  `zone_count`, `zone_keys`, `zone_of`, `zone_crack_tick`, `zone_fall_tick`,
  `zone_fall_offset`, `key_fall_tick`, `zone_phase`, `zone_count_for`, `config`.
  `cracked_keys`, `crack_progress` and `ticks_since_fall` now work per zone. The removal code
  is the same code as before, applied to one zone at a time. Events carry a `zone` field.
- `game/view/collapse_view.gd`: one drop root (`Zone<i>`) per zone, each with its own meshes,
  crack overlay and shader material. Each zone shudders, drops and hides on its own clock.
  The shader is unchanged.
- `game/view/level_mesher.gd`: new `PART_ZONE` part with a `zone_pieces` set (4th parameter).
  `""`, `PART_HUB` and `PART_REST` work exactly as before.
- `game/app/world.gd`: builds one mesher pass per zone and sets `lstate.collapse = _collapse`.
  Start, tick and `_collapse_sweep` wiring is unchanged; the sweep already worked off the
  removed keys.
- `game/sim/level_state.gd` + `game/sim/state_hash.gd`: new `collapse` field, and a new
  `COLLAPSE <phase> <start> <cur> <fallen_n> <removed_n>` dump line, documented in the header.
  The line is only written when a level carries a collapse state, so the bare sim, the bot and
  the replay goldens hash exactly as before.
- `game/tools/bot_run.gd`: comment updates only. See the bot section for why.
- `README.md` step 3 and `DECISIONS.md` (D-B10-1 added; D-B6-2 kept verbatim with a
  SUPERSEDED pointer, R9).

## The zone rule

Each non-hub piece's distance d is the XZ distance from the dais centre to the piece's NEAREST
tile centre (integer dx/dz). R is the largest d. The zone count is
N = clamp(ceil(R/16), 1, 6), and band = min(N-1, floor(d*N/R)). Empty bands are dropped. Zones
are stored in fall order, so zone 0 is the farthest. Zone i cracks on open + 900 i and falls on
open + 900 i + 300. Across the 90 measured levels that is 2 to 6 zones and 35 to 80 s in all.
The hub never falls.

## Tests (updated to assert the new design; nothing deleted, skipped or loosened)

`game/tests/test_platform_collapse.gd` was written for the global fall. It now asserts the
sequential design:

- leg 2: only zone 0 cracks on the open tick, and only its overlay shows.
- leg 3: at +300 exactly zone 0 is removed, the inner zones still stand and render, and nothing
  is cracked during the gap.
- legs 5, 8, 9 and 11: their pickers take the earliest zone that has a parkable tile, gift or
  mine, and wait for that zone's own fall tick. On seed 1 level 5 the outermost zone holds no
  mine and no parkable tile.

- leg 13: the full schedule, tick by tick, on 3 seeds/levels. It checks that the cracked set is
  exactly the current zone, that removed equals the fallen zones, that at most one overlay
  shows, that zones are whole pieces in outside-in order, and that the hub is intact at the end.
- leg 14: the numbers and the zone-count rule come from data, and a config override is honoured.
- leg 15: the COLLAPSE hash line changes on start and on the first fall, two identical runs hash
  the same, and there is no line when the level has no collapse.
- leg 16: the per-zone meshes add up to exactly the PART_REST mesh.

## Measurements

### 1. Unwinnability: 90 levels

Command: `<godot> --headless --path game --script res://out/zone_reach.gd` (scratch probe,
gitignored, in the style of `collapse_reach.gd`). Run speed is 9 u/s and distances are
straight XZ, as in the old check.

- **WIN rule**: the player picks the last black gift, and the run from it to the dais must
  finish before that gift's zone falls. Result: **0/90 unwinnable**. In 87/90 levels a real
  black gift sits on the hub and never falls. The worst of the other three still has 311.8 u
  of slack (seed 42 level 13). The farthest nearest-gift distance is 10.0 u.
- **STRAND rule** (stronger: from any tile the player might be standing on when the beacon
  opens): the straight distance to the nearest later-falling or hub tile must be covered
  before the tile's zone falls. Result: **0 of 91,770 tiles fail**. The worst slack is 11.0 u
  (seed 42 level 11, first zone). From any later zone the worst escape needs 40 u against the
  135 u that 15 s gives.

## Tried and reverted

- A first version of `_park_tile` and the gift/mine pickers used zone 0 only. Legs 5, 7, 9 and
  11 then found nothing on seed 1 level 5, so I generalised them to "the earliest zone that has
  one". The legs still check the exact fall tick.
- The negative-control edits to `collapse.json` were reverted, and the file was checked
  afterwards.

## Risks I am not fixing

1. **The bot does not simulate the collapse** (true since B6), so the bot gates cannot see it.
   The app-world driver shows what it costs: the bot no longer gets a free trip to the dais and
   dies to the cat on level 8. Making `bot_run.gd` run the collapse would make it more
   faithful, but it would be a separate ticket and would move the D-B9 baselines.
2. **The kiwi cat in the gold phase.** Enemies on the inner zones now live up to 80 s into the
   gold phase instead of 5 s. That is the design working as asked, but it makes the gold phase
   more dangerous (D-KIWI is already the bot's top killer).
3. Zone width 16 u and the cap of 6 are my choice, marked [CONTESTABLE] in D-B10-1. The worst
   strand slack (11 u) is what supports them. A wider zone or a lower cap shrinks it.
4. The reach checks use straight-line distance. They do not count jumps, height, or a path that
   has to go around.
5. Build cost: one mesher pass per zone (up to 6) instead of one PART_REST pass.
