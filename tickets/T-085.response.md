## 1. Tripmine — the deception rule (2.1)

T-021 already drew the box, the star, the particles, the turn and the 12 u boundary. What did not
conform, and now does:

| row | what the table says | what was wrong | what it does now |
|---|---|---|---|
| particles | **constant** — "the emitter never stops, including beyond 12 u" | `emitting = tell_visible(dist)`: the emitter switched OFF beyond 12 u | `emitting = true` always; only the alpha moves |
| particles / star | alpha 0 beyond 14 u → 1 at 10 u | no ramp at all (binary on/off at 12 u) | `particle_alpha()` / `star_alpha()`: linear 14 → 10 u, applied to the emitter's `color.a` and the star material's alpha |
| Method Acting | the ramp becomes 0 beyond 7 u → 1 at 5 u | absent | `set_method_acting(true)`; the radius is a parameter of this view, so it does not depend on `content/curses/method_acting.gd` (another worker's file) |
| star | six-pointed, `#FF4FD8`, emission 1.0, **on all four side faces** | baked into the model's single vertex-coloured primitive, top face only, never faded | `split_star()` cuts the pink triangles out of the .glb at load time into their own surface with a pink emissive material (D-T085-1); the four side-face hexagrams are view geometry |
| particles | 12/s, lifetime 1.5 s, size 0.06 u, `#FF4FD8`, rising 0.4 u/s, ± 0.2 u drift, emission 2.0 | 8 particles, 1.2 s, no velocity/emission spec | `amount 18 / lifetime 1.5` = 12/s, sphere spark 0.06 u, emission 2.0, velocity 0.4 u/s, drift volume 0.2 u (D-T085-2) |
| rim glow | identical to a black gift, `#7CFFF4` × 1.5, at **every** distance | already the gift view's material — but unproven | the test compares every parameter against a real black gift's overlay built by `gifts_view.gd` |
| trigger ring | faint `#FF4FD8` ground ring, radius 0.9 u, alpha 0.25, inside 12 u | absent | `TorusMesh` 0.9 u, alpha 0.25, visible inside 12 u |
| Radar Module: Mines | pink outline through fog within 14 u, regardless of the ramp | absent | `set_radar(true)`: a back-face pink shell, fog-exempt, visible at 14 u where the ramp is already 0 |
| body | 1 revolution per 4.0 s | already correct | asserted: Δrotation = TAU/4 per second |

## 2. Glitched floor (2.2)

T-037 built the decals (appear 12 ticks, 6-tick danger-red tear, 10 Hz expiry flicker, 12-tick
fade, `(x + z) mod 5` per-tile offset). Two rows were not conforming:

- **the 5-frame cycle at 8 fps** — the decal was pinned to one frame per tile. It now cycles:
  `frame = (offset + floor(age / 7.5)) mod 5` (8 fps = 7.5 ticks per frame, the table's "7–8 ticks
  per frame"). This is the gap the new test found.
- **ben's cleanse** — the decals vanished the instant the sim erased the tile. `on_cleanse()` now
  reads the sim's `glitched_cleanse` event (centre, radius, tick) and takes the decals inside 8 u
  away in an outward wave over 18 ticks: the wave front travels from the centre to r, and a tile
  loses its decal when the front reaches it.

The "checker underneath at 40% brightness" row is carried by the decal texture's own alpha, not by
the view's modulate (D-T085-5) — the modulate alpha is the appear/fade ramp that T-037's test pins
at 1.0, and the `glitchfloor_00..04` PNGs are TBD placeholders.

## 3. Beacon (2.3)

`beacon_view.gd` was frame-driven (`_process` + `Time.get_ticks_msec`), so the column could not
appear on a tick. It is now tick-driven:

- `set_open(opened, tick)` records the open tick; `sync_tick(tick)` computes the ramp and the pulse
  from the tick. The ramp starts at 1/36, so **the column is visible on the open tick itself**
  (D-T085-3) — the done-when's "appears on the tick of the last real black gift". The test drives
  the real sim: two real black gifts collected on ticks 100 and 140, `beacon.open(140)`, and the
  column is up at 140, 60 u tall at 176.
- the 0.5 Hz pulse (0.9 → 1.0) is now a function of the tick, not of wall-clock time.
- **new**: the white shock ring that crosses the hub over the same 36 ticks.
- **new**: the blocked state — `set_blocked(true, slow_door_ticks)` turns the column danger red
  `#FF2A2A` and shows the Slow Door ring filling from the axis to the 1.5 u trigger radius over 180
  ticks.
- the door slab is not built: the T-015 beacon mesh has no doorway (D-T020-6 stands, D-T085-4).

## 4. Respawn (2.5)

`game/view/respawn_view.gd` is new. It reads the sim's `respawn` event and the controller's
invulnerability countdown:

- **reappear**: a vertical white streak 3 u tall that collapses onto the tile over 9 ticks, and a
  ground ring expanding to 1.5 u over the same 9 ticks.
- **invulnerability**: 60 ticks (the sim's `respawn_invuln` 1.0 s — the test asserts the view's
  constant equals `round(consts["respawn_invuln"] * 60)` and that the sim's own `invuln -= dt`
  countdown reaches 0 on tick 60). The blink is a tick function: 8 Hz = 7.5 ticks per cycle, half
  period 3.75 ticks, phase measured from the respawn tick (D-T085-6). The white rim (a back-face
  shell around the player figure) is drawn while the countdown is > 0 and **gone on the tick it
  reaches 0** — the test asserts alpha 1 at 1 tick left and alpha 0 with the node hidden at 0.
- "death at 0 charges: no streak, no reappear" is the caller's contract: it never calls
  `on_respawn()` on that path.

## The test

`game/tests/test_vfx.gd` (TESTS 45 → 46). Six groups, one per table. Negative-tested — each of
these planted defects fails the build:

| planted defect | result |
|---|---|

| N2 the ramp's far end 14 u → 12 u | CAUGHT — "particle alpha 0.0000 at 12 u, want 0.5" |
| N3 the beacon ramp starts at 0 on the open tick | CAUGHT — "no column on the tick of the last real black gift" |
| N4 the rim survives the end tick (`>= 0` instead of `> 0`) | CAUGHT — "rim alpha 1.00 on the tick invulnerability ends, want 0" |
| N5 the decal frame stops cycling | CAUGHT — "the decal did not advance one frame after 8 ticks at 8 fps" |

## What I could not do, and the readings I took

- **The beacon's door slab** (2.3 opening row) is not built: the T-015 beacon mesh has no doorway
  and Blender is not in this environment (D-T085-4). The other three clauses of that row — the
  emission ramp, the column rise, the shock ring — are built.
- **The glitched floor's "on the player" row** (a thin glitch vignette at the screen edge and the
  class-ability icon struck through) is HUD work: `view/hud/screen_fx.gd` and the ability icon are
  T-086's surface, and the sim already exposes the state (`controller.on_glitched_floor()`). Not
  touched here.
- **The beacon's "entering" row** (screen fade to `#E8FFFF` over 18 ticks, the HUD pip restore) is
  the same HUD surface (`screen_fx.white_bloom()` + `void_pips`), not this ticket's view.
- **Radar Module: Mines** — the view hook (`set_radar`) is built and tested; the upgrade file that
  would call it is being written by another worker, so nothing wires it yet.
- **Method Acting** — the view takes the shortened ramp as a parameter (`set_method_acting`), so it
  does not depend on `content/curses/method_acting.gd` existing.
- **The star's ramp** needed the .glb split at load time (D-T085-1): the model is one
  vertex-coloured primitive and Blender is absent, so the star could not be faded any other way
  without a shader.
- **The 40% checker** under the glitched decal is the texture's alpha, not a view constant
  (D-T085-5).
