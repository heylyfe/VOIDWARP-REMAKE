# B4 — the enemy spawn pays the whole plan on one tick; spread it across ticks — DONE

The spawn now pays at most `SPAWN_PER_TICK = 4` bodies per tick, in plan order, and the plan
completes by `spawn_complete_tick()`. The worst step on the biggest level fell from 15.66 ms to
7.82 ms against the 16.67 ms budget (FLEET_PACK.md A13). The D-056 rule, the 25 u floor, SR-3
and the anchor-kind preference are untouched; only the tick each body appears on moved.

## 2. What changed

`game/sim/enemy_runtime.gd`:

- `const SPAWN_PER_TICK := 4` (D-B4-1), with the measurement that chose it in the comment.
- `plan_copies_total` counts every copy the plan ever held; `spawn_complete_tick()` returns
  `spawn_delay_ticks + ceil(P / SPAWN_PER_TICK) - 1` - the bound, stated in the code.
- `_spawn_planned()` takes a per-tick budget consumed in plan order. An entry that runs out of
  budget mid-way keeps its `spawned_copies` progress and its place in the queue, so the next
  tick resumes exactly where it stopped. D-T027-4's deferral is unchanged: an entry with no
  legal tile still waits, and the entries behind it are still attempted.

The RULE is untouched: `pick_spawn_tile`, `_pick_anchor`, `_pick_round_trip`, `_copy_positions`
and `_round_trip_cached` are byte-identical. A body spawned on a later tick is picked by the
same rule against that tick's inputs.

The schedule is a pure function of the plan and the tick - a counter consumed in plan order.
No wall clock, no frame time, no new randomness, no new RNG stream.

## 4. Tests

`game/tests/test_spawn_rule.gd` - two new legs, and the four existing legs unchanged:

- `_spread_and_bound`: 3 entries x 3 copies on a synthetic line, spawn delay 10. Asserts
  nothing before tick 10; exactly `SPAWN_PER_TICK` bodies after the first spawn tick and fewer
  than the plan (the spread); the plan complete at the bound tick and NOT before it; the spawn
  order is plan order (vote order, then copy index); every body >= 25 u from the player; copies
  of one entry >= 30 u apart (SR-3); copy 0 of every entry on the preferred nook. The budget
  cuts MID-ENTRY here (entry 1's third copy waits a tick), which the body count proves.
- `_bound_on_a_real_level`: seed 7 level 30, a 22-body plan, spawn delay 30. Asserts the plan
  is complete by `spawn_complete_tick()` (tick 35), that it was NOT complete on the first spawn
  tick, that the queue is empty at the bound, and re-asserts the 25 u floor and SR-3 on the
  real level's picks.

Not vacuous: with `SPAWN_PER_TICK` set to 99 (no budget) both legs fail -

```
FAIL test_spawn_rule.gd: spread: 10 bodies after the first spawn tick, want 99 (SPAWN_PER_TICK)
FAIL test_spawn_rule.gd: spread: the whole plan (10) landed on the first spawn tick - not spread
FAIL test_spawn_rule.gd: bound: the whole plan (22) landed on the first spawn tick - not spread
```

The existing legs needed no change: `_separation_and_round_trip` plans 2 copies (inside one
tick's budget) and `_deferral_keeps_the_plan` plans an unsatisfiable level, so both still assert
exactly what they asserted before. No assertion was weakened or removed.

Determinism: `test_replay.gd` (the 60-tick checkpoint replay, the perturbation leg and the ten
recorded bot runs) is green in the suite below. The spawn schedule adds no state that is not a
function of the plan and the tick.

## 6. Deviations and open items

- The ticket's 86 ms baseline did not reproduce; the measured baseline was 15.66 ms. I did not
  move any threshold to make this look better - the budget is still 16.67 ms and the numbers
  above are the same tool that produced the 86 ms figure.
- `SPAWN_PER_TICK = 4` is [CONTESTABLE] (D-B4-1): the arrival is now spread over 6 ticks
  (0.1 s) for a 22-body plan instead of landing on one. If the arrival is meant to read as a
  single event, the answer is a view-side flourish on the first tick, not a larger N.
- The bound assumes a legal tile exists on every tick (D-T027-4's deferral is the only thing
  that can push a body past it). On real levels step (c) always finds one, which the real-level
  leg asserts; on a synthetic level with no legal tile the plan still defers forever, exactly
  as D-T027-4 says it must.
- The remaining worst step on the mines level (9.44 ms at tick 364) is not a spawn cost: that
  level carries 29 mines and the per-tick hazard-footprint pass is O(mines). Out of scope here.
