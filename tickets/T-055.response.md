## What was built

- `tools/synth_audio.py` - stdlib-only (`wave` + `math` + `json`) synthesizer.
  Scans `game/content/enemies/*/enemy.json` (`stats.audio_cues` lists and
  `stats.audio` dict values, utf-8-sig for the BOM'd files) and synthesises one
  distinct placeholder `.wav` per distinct cue name. `python tools/synth_audio.py`
  synthesises + verifies; `python tools/synth_audio.py --check` verifies only
  (used by nothing yet; for humans and T-087). Deterministic LCG noise, byte-stable
  rebuilds, peak-normalised to 0.89, 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit (section 3's format).

  - the 24 cues referenced by the twelve enemy.json files (kiwi_cat meow /

  - the 6 Isagi/nuke cues the repo names elsewhere (TBD.md T-033 rows:
    isagi_voice_direct_shot, isagi_shoot_sae_math_final_shot, isagi_gunshot;
    nuke sprites.json TBD audio hooks: lock_on_beep, rocket_booster_roar,
