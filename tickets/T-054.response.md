## Why no biome's light colours were adjusted upward

The ticket's clause triggers only "if a biome fails". Against the authoritative section 12.3
check, none failed. The section 10 INFO row cannot be fixed by ANY upward lightness adjustment,
of the platforms or anything else in a biome's palette:

- With the fixed canon tell #FF4FD8 (DESIGN.md section 10; every biome file's _doc: the tell
  never changes; TERRAIN_SPEC section 12.2 "the only pink in the level"), even a pure WHITE
  platform_light at 1.35 total radiance reaches only 2.675-2.703 vs the tell at 40 u (measured
  with fog_check.gd's own math; max over all five biomes).
- For verdigris (plat lum 0.279), violet_hour (0.218) and indigo_trench (0.193) the platform
  is DARKER than the tell (0.318); raising lightness shrinks the ratio toward 1.0, and passing
  above the tell needs plat lum >= 1.054 (impossible). Darkening is the only direction that
  raises their contrast.
- Darkening far enough to reach 3.0 (fogged plat lum <= 0.026-0.033 at 40 u) puts the fogged
  platform at 60 u below ~0.033, breaking section 12.3's required "platform_light vs fog @60 >=
  3.0" row, which needs fogged lum >= 0.107-0.117 in every biome. The two canon rows are
  mutually exclusive for a fixed #FF4FD8.
- Lowering density or fog brightness is outside this ticket's brief (fog density and fog colour
  are the biome's act contract; section 12.3's 3.0/4.5 thresholds are authoritative).

So the finding is parked (PARKED.md row 11) with the owner rulings it invites, exactly as the
standing clause requires; nothing was swept under the rug, no threshold moved, no canon hex
moved. The ticket's own done-when ("5/5 pass") is met against the check the brief names as
authoritative (FOGCHECK OK, 250 checks) - but the owner should know section 10's 3.0 row is
unmeetable as written.
