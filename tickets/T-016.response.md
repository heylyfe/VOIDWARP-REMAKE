## 4. DEVIATIONS / ASSUMPTIONS

- fps values and the Claude/Isagi frame recipes are not in any spec; they are single
  call-site numbers in the pipeline, marked [CONTESTABLE] inside the code (D-T016-1/-2).
- The gift Larper "light" is implemented as a central lamp disc (radius 18% of the body
  width) whose brightness pulses 60/80/100/80/60%; pulsing the whole body instead would
  sink the dimmest frames below the A8 fog threshold (D-T016-2, D-T016-4).
- The fog contrast of TERRAIN_SPEC 12.3 is computed in linear colour space; on the sRGB
  reading even pure magenta fails 3.0 against near-black fog at 40 u (D-T016-4).
- The clawd Crab plushie sprite (URL in ENEMIES.md) is not fetched; Claude's frames are the
  logo-based placeholder, marked TBD in TBD.md for later replacement.
- One `@warning_ignore("integer_division")` suppresses Godot's integer-division warnings in
  the pixel-index loops (no behavioural effect).
