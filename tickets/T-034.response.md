## 2. The behavior (DESIGN.md section 5.5, verbatim numbers; D-064 salvo + blocking)

Phase loop, 60 Hz, all numbers read from `enemy.json` `stats` (never inlined in code):

1. TRACK (150 ticks = 2.5 s): a ground ring (radius 5 u) follows the locked player, the lock-on
   beep rising 2 -> 8 Hz over the window; the LOS ray - 12 u high, 20 u out, direction fixed per
   salvo (D-T-034-1, [CONTESTABLE]) - is checked every tick: blocked 30 ticks CONTINUOUSLY (the
   0.5 s) breaks the lock.
2. Broken lock: the ring holds stopped where it broke, 120 ticks (2.0 s), then tracking restarts.
   A restart NEVER resets the salvo budget (the sim's SalvoClock: start() on an ACTIVE clock is a
   no-op).
3. LOCK (90 ticks = 1.5 s, THE SAME 90): the locked ring fills rim-inward while the missile
   descends from 30 u above the ring centre (1/3 u per tick). A missile meeting any prop, spire
   pad or tile slab/step riser in its column detonates THERE (on a spire pad: the pad's top,
   D-064).
4. Blast: a sphere of radius 5 u around the detonation point; the nearest player inside dies with
   code D-NUKE (nearest-only: PARKED row 6, the kill API is per-player).
5. PAUSE: 300 ticks (5.0 s), then repeat. After a harmless EXPIRY the pause starts with the anim
   state recover (no attack_explosion draws for an expiry that killed nobody).

D-064 salvo life: tracking including EVERY restart after a broken lock plus the descent lasts at
most 360 ticks (6.0 s) from the FIRST tracking tick. Expiry is harmless (no lock, no descent, no
blast), the 5.0 s pause starts, and then a FRESH salvo begins with a legitimately restarted budget
(D-T-034-3). The anim side maps phases to the anim block's state ids (D-T-034-4).
