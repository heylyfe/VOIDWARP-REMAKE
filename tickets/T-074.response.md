## What landed

- `game/sim/offers.gd` - the D-074 draw: an E bay offers one NEW altar (eligible,
  0 votes, lowest first-shown min_level first, ties by seeded draw), one REPEAT
  altar (in play, below its cap, never the type of the previous vote) and one
  FREE altar (seeded pick from everything eligible and not yet shown this bay);
  an empty category's slot becomes a FREE pick; a pick that would push the chaser
  load past the ceiling is not drawn; Hunger fallback unchanged (also fires when
  the ceiling starves every remaining type). Altars carry `"slot"`; the returned
  shape keeps `"kind": "enemy"`, so the view and `_select_enemy` semantics are
  untouched.

- `game/core/schemas/offering_schedule.schema.json` - the schema for that block.
- `game/tests/test_offers.gd` - the 1,000-run walk + a uniform-random-voter

  legible question intends (NEW altar first, else seeded uniform). Under a naive
  uniform-random voter the coverage falls (measured 8.9% on the same seeds) - the

  the player decides. Kept as informational only (D-T074-6).

  headroom) had fewer than 3 types left; that is the T-023 draw's own shape rule
  ("up to 3"), not a starvation - zero Hunger draws fired across all runs.
