  2 u of an existing plushie; pickup within 0.9 u horizontally and 2.0 u vertically

- D-T040-7: the spawn fade zeroes the row's hit diameter for its 30 ticks, so the
  framework's contact check cannot kill during the fade (the anim block's spawn row:
