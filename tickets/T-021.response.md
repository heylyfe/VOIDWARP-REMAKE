## Decisions taken (full text in DECISIONS.md, D-T021-1..6)

- D-T021-1: the mine's point of record is the slot's tile centre
  `(x+0.5, z+0.5)`; the box point lifts it to the section 6.7 occupancy height
  and the blast measures from there (3D sphere, r = 3) [CONTESTABLE: no
  document says horizontal vs 3D for the blast; contact is horizontal by spec].
- D-T021-2: no collision prop is added - the 0.9 u trigger cylinder IS the
  wall; the walkable bypass (D-026 generator guarantee) stays the only way past.
- D-T021-3: the consumed bitset joins the replay hash (`MINES` line).
- D-T021-4: a blast victim runs shield -> Last Light -> death; the defuse roll
  is never re-rolled for victims (it is fixed per (seed, level, slot)).
- D-T021-5: an invulnerable player (respawn / Last Light window) neither
  triggers the ladder nor consumes the mine (DESIGN.md 3.1: tripmines do not
  kill during respawn invulnerability).
- D-T021-6: the view's turn rate is 1 rev / 4.0 s from DESIGN.md 3.3 (the
  sprite pipeline's fps 10 in sprites.json is a pipeline default, not the
  playback rate); the emitter + rim implement the tell.

  not exist yet, so the parameter defaults to "not tracked" (-1).
